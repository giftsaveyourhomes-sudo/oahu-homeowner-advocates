FAQ

Questions homeowners ask most

Is your help really free?

Yes. Our homeowner support and resource connections are 100% free of charge for homeowners. There's no obligation and no pressure to sell.

Do I have to sell my home?

No. Understanding your options comes first — keeping the home, pausing foreclosure, negotiating, or selling only if you choose. No one should make that decision without knowing all the alternatives.

What should I bring (or tell you) on the first call?

If you have them: any foreclosure notice, your loan servicer or lender's name, court case information, auction date, HOA or tax letters, and your best contact details. If you don't have documents, that's okay — we'll start with what you know.

Are you lawyers or HUD counselors?

No. We are homeowner advocates. We share general information to help you understand your situation and options. We don't provide legal or tax advice. When those services are needed, we connect you with trusted, verified professionals.

If there's an urgent deadline, what should I do?

Call or text one of us as soon as possible so we can confirm dates and identify the fastest next steps. If you're in immediate danger of losing housing, contact a HUD-approved housing counselor or Legal Aid right away.

Important information

Not professional advice

The members of Oʻahu Homeowner Advocates are homeowner advocates and are not licensed attorneys, mortgage professionals, financial advisors, tax advisors, or HUD-approved housing counselors. Information provided through this website or in person is general and educational only and is not legal, financial, tax, or real estate advice. No attorney-client or fiduciary relationship is created by using this site or contacting us.

Free to homeowners

We do not charge homeowners fees or request advance payments, and we do not take any portion of your money or property. In some cases, a member of our team may be interested in purchasing a home if the homeowner chooses to sell — this is always disclosed upfront, and you are never obligated to sell to anyone.

Urgent situations

If you are facing an imminent foreclosure sale or court deadline, contact a licensed Hawaiʻi attorney, the Legal Aid Society of Hawaiʻi, or a HUD-approved housing counselor immediately. Do not rely on this website in place of timely professional help.
