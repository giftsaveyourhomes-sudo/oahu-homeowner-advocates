Questions people ask us

Why is this free?

Because we believe every family deserves to understand their options before making a big decision about their home. Homeowners never pay us anything — not for the first call, and not later.

Will you make me sell my house?

No. Selling is only one of many possible paths, and it's your decision alone. Our job is to make sure you know every option first — including the ones that keep you in your home.

What should I have ready for our first talk?

Anything you have is helpful: letters from your lender or the court, the name of your loan servicer, an auction or court date, HOA or tax notices. If you have nothing in hand, that's fine too — we'll start with what you remember.

Are you attorneys or HUD counselors?

No. We're homeowner advocates who share general information so you understand where you stand. We don't give legal or tax advice, and when you need that kind of help, we'll point you to trusted professionals.

My auction date is close. Is it too late?

Reach out right away so we can look at your dates together and find the quickest next steps. If you're about to lose your housing, also contact a HUD-approved housing counselor or the Legal Aid Society of Hawaiʻi today.

The fine print

We're advocates, not advisors

The members of 808 Home Help are homeowner advocates. We are not licensed attorneys, mortgage professionals, financial or tax advisors, or HUD-approved housing counselors. Anything we share on this website or in conversation is general information for education only, not legal, financial, tax, or real estate advice. Using this site or contacting us does not create an attorney-client or fiduciary relationship.

No cost to homeowners

We never charge homeowners fees, ask for money up front, or take any part of your money or property. In some situations, a member of our team may be interested in buying a home if the owner decides to sell. If that ever applies, we tell you up front, and you are never obligated to sell to anyone.

If a deadline is near

If a foreclosure sale or court date is coming up soon, contact a licensed Hawaiʻi attorney, the Legal Aid Society of Hawaiʻi, or a HUD-approved housing counselor right away. Please don't rely on this website in place of timely professional help.

Your privacy

Messages sent through our form are delivered by a third-party service (Formspree) and used only to respond to you. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.
